The Property Buying Process

A step-by-step roadmap to secure your dream home with confidence and legal safety.

1. Budget Planning

Use the EMI Calculator above to establish strict affordability limits and secure your initial down payment reserves.

2. Property Search

Leverage the 'Location Analytics' map to filter regions by price per sqft, amenities, and predicted growth.

3. Site Visit & Verification

Physically inspect the build quality. Crucially, verify builder credibility, RERA registration, and clear land titles.

4. Loan Pre-Approval

Submit income documetation to banks. Compare processing fees and finalize the lowest interest rate offer.

5. Agreement & Booking

Pay the token booking amount. Draft and sign the 'Agreement to Sell' outlining specific possession timelines.

6. Registration & Possession

Execute the final Sale Deed at the registrar. Pay Stamp Duty & clearances, then receive the keys to your property!
