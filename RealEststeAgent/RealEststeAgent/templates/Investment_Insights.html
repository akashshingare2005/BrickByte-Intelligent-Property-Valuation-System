Investment Insights Dashboard

Analyze EstateIQ investment prospects and understand how property features affect pricing.

1. Average Property Price by City

Insight: This chart compares the average property prices across different cities. It helps identify which real estate markets are the most expensive or affordable overall.

2. Property Price Distribution

Insight: The histogram displays how market prices are distributed. A skewed distribution indicates whether most properties fall into affordable or luxury segments.

3. Properties by BHK Type

Insight: This pie chart shows the market share of different BHK configurations. It highlights what type of properties are most commonly available for buyers.

4. Price Range by BHK

Insight: Box plots reveal the median, spread, and outliers of prices for each BHK category. It shows how much variation in price exists within the same home size.

5. Area vs. Market Price

Insight: This scatter plot shows the direct correlation between a property's total square footage and its price. Generally, larger areas command higher market values.

6. Price vs. Property Age

Insight: The line chart tracks how the age of a property impacts its average price. It helps buyers understand depreciation trends over time.

7. Numeric Feature Correlation Heatmap

Insight: The heatmap visualizes the statistical relationship between all numerical features. A score closer to 1 or -1 means a strong positive or negative correlation with the property price.

8. Parking by Locality Tier

Insight: This stacked bar chart compares parking availability across Budget, Mid, and Premium locality tiers, indicating if premium areas guarantee more parking.

9. Area vs. Price (Bubble=Bathrooms)

Insight: A 3-dimensional view where x is area, y is price, and bubble size is the number of bathrooms. It shows how three features aggregate together.

10. Properties by Furnishing Status

Insight: This count plot displays the total volume of Unfurnished, Semi-Furnished, and Fully-Furnished properties in the market available to buyers.
